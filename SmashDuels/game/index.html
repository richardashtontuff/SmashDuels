HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

There are no health bars. Every hit raises your damage %. The higher your %, the further you fly. Knock opponents past the invisible blast zones around the stage to take a stock.

The higher your damage, the more knockback you take. Wider side and upper blast zones give you more room to recover.

SMASH ATTACKS

Tap a direction and press attack at the same time for a chargeable smash attack. Hold the attack button to charge for extra damage.

Tilt gently (hold a direction) and press attack for a quick tilt. In the air, direction + attack gives you neutrals, forwards, backs, ups and spikes (down-air).

FIGHTER SPECIALS

Press Special for a fighter's signature move, or hold left / right and press Special for their side special. Running + Attack uses their dash attack. Up + Special or jump + Special is your recovery; down + Special uses a defensive move.

Keyboard bindings can be changed in Settings. Try the Tutorial for guided practice.

Valor chains crescent cuts, launches with Skyslash Rush, and follows up a connecting Skyward Ascent with extra strikes. Grim strikes a wider area around him and charges like a boulder. Astra calls falling stars and leaves a comet trail. Vesper chains rapid blades and blinks through foes. Rook strings together punches and rises into a launching strike. Marrow holds Special to charge Soul Reap (up to 2× damage after one second), then releases a spectral blast that heals on a clean hit. Up + Special spins his axe in a rising circle.

DEFENSE

Shield blocks attacks but not grabs, and it shrinks as it takes hits. If it breaks, you're stunned.

Shield + down spot-dodges. Holding left or right while shielding keeps your shield up. Shield in the air is an air dodge. Press shield as you land from a tumble to tech.

RECOVERY

Off-stage? Use your double jump and one Up + Special or Jump + Special recovery per airtime. Landing or grabbing a ledge restores it. Catch a free ledge from outside; hold toward the stage or press up to climb, jump to leap, attack to get up attacking, shield to roll, or down to drop.

Grabs beat shields. Shields beat attacks. Attacks beat grabs.

ITEMS & FINAL SMASH

Items fall onto the stage — weapons, throwables, healing, growth mushrooms, stars, traps and summon sigils. Walk into one to pick it up. Press Grab / Use Item (V for P1) to fire a ranged item, throw a bomb, shell or bumper, or drop a melee weapon. Melee weapons enhance your normal Attack moves (F for P1): the beam sword extends reach, the bat adds launch power, the star rod fires stars, and the hammer hits harder. Healing, status items and summons activate on contact; containers break open on contact.

Smash the glowing Smash Ball until it shatters, then press Special to unleash your Final Smash.
